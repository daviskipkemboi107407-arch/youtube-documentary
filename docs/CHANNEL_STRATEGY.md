# Channel Strategy
## Niche
Mysteries + History + Science Documentaries
## Channel Format
A faceless documentary channel built around compelling stories, unanswered questions, historical mysteries, scientific discoveries, strange phenomena, and fascinating real-world events.
## Content Principles
- Evidence-driven
- Original
- Story-first
- Curiosity-driven
- High-quality research
- Strong narrative structure
- Visually engaging
- Honest about uncertainty
- No fabricated facts
- No low-effort mass-produced AI content
## Core Content Areas
### Mysteries
Unexplained events, strange cases, lost places, mysterious discoveries, unusual phenomena, and questions that remain genuinely interesting.
### History
Forgotten events, historical mysteries, hidden stories, unusual people, civilizations, disasters, discoveries, and events that changed the world.
### Science
Scientific mysteries, discoveries, experiments, strange natural phenomena, space, archaeology, biology, physics, technology, and the science behind extraordinary questions.
## Storytelling Standard
Every documentary should begin with a compelling question or mystery and lead the viewer through evidence, competing explanations, discoveries, and conclusions.
The goal is not simply to provide information.
The goal is to make the viewer think:
"Wait... what actually happened?"
## Production Philosophy
Create one excellent documentary first.
Learn the complete production process manually.
Document the process.
Identify repetitive work.
Then progressively systemize and automate validated processes.
Automation must increase efficiency without sacrificing research quality, factual accuracy, originality, or storytelling.
## Business Direction
Build the channel as a long-term media asset.
Potential revenue streams include:
- YouTube advertising
- Sponsorships
- Affiliate revenue
- Digital products
- Other complementary business opportunities
